# Challenge Brief

_Full rules: `RULES.md`._

## Challenge: "Fresh off the frontier"
Build a project that uses a technology, dataset, or discovery first made publicly available within the past 12 months (i.e. on or after ~Oct 23, 2025) to solve a problem, explore an idea, or create an experience in a field you care about. Bringing your own field (biology, music, history, ...) is encouraged.

The new technology or resource must be **central** to what we build. Be ready to show:
- what it does
- what difficulties we faced
- what makes the technology or discovery recent

## Our idea: one-stop voter education
Interactive map: a voter enters their ZIP code and sees every election they're eligible to vote in during the next cycle (Nov 3, 2026 general election). They can browse every race, see all candidates, and get the key information they need to decide on each one.

## Judging criteria
Impact · Technical Execution · Design & Usability · Presentation & Demo (+ Novelty per official site)
