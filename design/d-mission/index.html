How it works

From address to answers in three passes

  1. Locate

    Your address resolves to congressional, state, county and judicial districts, then to the exact races on your ballot.

  2. Verify

    Candidate lists come from official election records, in the order the state prints them. We link you to your official sample ballot.

  3. Research

    A Gemini Deep Research agent reads dozens of sources, then writes a cited, side-by-side comparison you can audit line by line.

BallotScout is nonpartisan and unaffiliated with any campaign or party. AI-generated research may contain errors; verify with the cited sources and your Supervisor of Elections.
