      2026 General Election Voter Guide: Representative in Congress, Florida District 3

      Verified

      The November 3, 2026, general election for Florida's 3rd Congressional District features a three-way contest to represent a diverse geographic constituency spanning North Central Florida. The district boundaries encompass Alachua, Baker, Bradford, Columbia, Dixie, Gilchrist, Hamilton, Lafayette, Levy, Marion, Suwannee, and Union counties . Historically, the district has leaned conservative, carrying a Cook Partisan Voter Index (CPVI) of R+10, which indicates a voting electorate that performs ten percentage points more Republican than the national average .

      This voter guide provides a neutral overview of the three candidates on the general election ballot: incumbent Representative Kat Cammack (Republican), challenger Seth Harp (Democratic), and challenger Mike Klein (No Party Affiliation) . The profiles are structured identically, covering each candidate's background, stated positions, public record, campaign finance filings and endorsements.
